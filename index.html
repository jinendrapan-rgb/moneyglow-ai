FAQ

Questions, answered

Is my financial data uploaded anywhere?

No. MoneyGlow calculates everything on your device and there's no backend server storing your data. Backups are encrypted on your device before they go anywhere you choose.

Do I need an AI key to use it?

No. The Debt Optimizer, Health Score and Decision Lab work fully offline. A key is only needed for the optional AI Copilot chat and plan generation — and it's your own key, removable any time.

Is it really free?

Yes — the free plan is free forever. Premium adds real AI chat, PIN / Face ID, encrypted backup, unlimited goals, net worth and the bill calendar.

How do I pay for Premium?

Premium is a monthly subscription, paid by USDT (TRC20) or UAE bank transfer and verified manually. There's no card processor and no automatic renewal — if you stop paying, Premium ends after that month and the free features keep working.

Is there lifetime access?

No. Premium and Founder are both monthly subscriptions. Founder members keep the AED 19 price for as long as their subscription stays active.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. There's no bank sync — you enter or import your numbers yourself, which is exactly why nothing has to leave your device.

Is this financial advice?

No. MoneyGlow is a calculation and decision-support tool built on the numbers you enter. It isn't regulated financial advice and there's no human advisor behind the AI.
