Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Effective immediately

💜 We want you to be completely happy with MoneyGlow AI Premium. If you're not satisfied, we'll make it right.

1. Our Refund Guarantee

We offer a 7-day full refund on MoneyGlow AI Premium monthly payments, no questions asked. If you paid for a month of Premium and are not satisfied for any reason, contact us within 7 days of that payment and we will refund it in full.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Email us at hello.moneyglowai@gmail.com
  • Include your payment reference (MG-…) or the email you used
  • We will process your refund within 3–5 business days

3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are available if:

  • Your request is made within 7 days of the payment
  • You have not already received a refund for that month
  • The payment was made through the app's official USDT or bank-transfer instructions

4. After a Refund

Once a refund is processed, your Premium access will be deactivated. Your locally stored financial data will remain on your device and will not be deleted. You may continue using the free features of MoneyGlow AI.

5. Technical Issues

If you are experiencing technical issues with Premium features, please contact us at hello.moneyglowai@gmail.com before requesting a refund — we will do our best to resolve the issue promptly.

6. Payment Methods

MoneyGlow AI does not use a card processor. Premium is paid monthly by USDT (TRC20) or UAE bank transfer and verified manually. Refunds go back the same way: USDT to the wallet you paid from, or a bank transfer to the account you paid from. Bank processing can take 3–7 business days after we send it.

7. Subscription, not lifetime

Premium is a monthly subscription with no automatic renewal. There is no lifetime access. If you stop paying, Premium ends at the end of the paid month and the free features keep working.

8. Contact

For any refund questions, email hello.moneyglowai@gmail.com — we typically respond within 24 hours.
